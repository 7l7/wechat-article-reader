# Article Lens — Terms of Service

**Last updated:** 12 November 2026

Article Lens is an independent third-party tool; it is not affiliated with Tencent or WeChat.

## Purpose
Article Lens helps users read and work with publicly accessible WeChat Official Account article content. It also processes HTML and Markdown that users voluntarily provide.

## Permitted use
Use the tool only for content you are entitled to access and process. Respect content owners' rights, applicable laws and the WeChat platform's access restrictions. You are responsible for your use of resulting text and links.

## Availability and limitations
Public pages may become unavailable, require verification, change format or restrict automated access. Extraction may be incomplete. The service does not guarantee that any specific URL can be read, that all embedded media is captured, or that retrieved content is complete or error-free. You should verify important information against the original source.

## No account access
The service does not log into WeChat, handle payments, purchase goods, publish articles or modify any WeChat account. Do not supply passwords or session cookies.

## Acceptable use
Do not abuse the endpoint, flood it with requests, attempt to circumvent access controls, or use it for unlawful collection or distribution of content. Requests may be rate-limited or blocked for safety and reliability.

The service and these terms may change. For support, report an issue at https://github.com/7l7/wechat-article-reader/issues.
